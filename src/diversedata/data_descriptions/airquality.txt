Air Quality

This database contains information on air quality in Vancouver, including particulate matter,
greenhouse gases, and more, at hourly resolution. This data is available on Open Meteo, and
is compiled from multiple sources, including the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service
(CAMS) ENSEMBLE data provider. The data can be accessed through the
[Open Meteo website](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api) or via API call under the CC BY 4.0 data licence.

Variables
----------
time : Date and time of day
day_of_week : Day of the week
hour : Hour of day
is_weekend : Boolean True/False for weekend day versus weekday
pm10 : Concentration of particulate matter with diameter smaller than 10 µm (PM10) close to surface (10 meter above ground)
pm2_5 : Concentration of particulate matter with diameter smaller than 2.5 µm (PM2.5) close to surface (10 meter above ground)
nitrogen_dioxide : Concentration of the atmospheric gas nitrogen dioxide close to surface (10 meter above ground)
us_aqi : United States Air Quality Index (AQI) calculated for different particulate matter and gases individually. The consolidated us_aqi returns the maximum of all individual indices. Ranges from 0-50 (good), 50-100 (moderate), 100-150 (unhealthy for sensitive groups), 150-200 (unhealthy), 200-300 (very unhealthy) and 300-500 (hazardous). 

Source
------
Open Meteo via API 
https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api

Examples
--------
>>> import diversedata as dd
>>> df = dd.load_data("air quality")
>>> df.head()
                time pm10 pm2_5 nitrogen_dioxide us_aqi hour day_of_week is_weekend
0 2023-09-01 00:00:00 22.9  14.9             10.4     57    0      Friday      FALSE

>>> dd.print_data_description("air quality")
